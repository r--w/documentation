- Added `circulating_supply` to `/tickers/{coin_id}/historical` and `/coins/{coin_id}/ohlcv/historical`, `/ohlcv/latest`, `/ohlcv/today`, available on Business, Ultimate and Enterprise (derived as market cap / price, an approximation)
